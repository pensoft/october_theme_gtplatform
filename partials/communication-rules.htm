Communication rules

1Respect others

Treat every member with courtesy. Challenge ideas, not people, and keep the conversation constructive and welcoming.

2No harassment

Harassment, hate speech, threats or personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated and may lead to removal from the platform.

3No spam

Avoid repetitive posts, irrelevant links and self-promotion. Keep each topic focused and on-subject.

4No propaganda

This space is for knowledge exchange, not political or commercial propaganda. Keep contributions factual and relevant.
